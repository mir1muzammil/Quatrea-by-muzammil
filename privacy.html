Privacy policy.

Last updated 7 November 2026. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

If you send us a message through the contact form, your email app opens with the details you typed (name, optional phone number, interest and message). We only receive what you choose to send.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are useful. It sets cookies only after you press Accept in the cookie notice, and we ask Google to anonymise IP addresses. If you press Decline, no analytics script is loaded. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so we do not ask again.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry, usually within 24 hours.
  • To arrange consults, samples and orders you ask for.
  • To improve this website using anonymous, aggregated statistics.

We do not sell personal data and we do not send marketing email unless you ask us to.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the business, such as couriers delivering an order, and only what they need to do that job. Embedded Google Maps on the contact page is provided by Google and is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Retention

Enquiry emails are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted. Order records are kept as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Write to hello@quatrea.example and we will respond within 24 hours.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Questions? Contact us. This page describes a fictional brand concept and is sample text, so have it reviewed before using it for a real business.
