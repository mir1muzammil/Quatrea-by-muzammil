Questions, answered.

Everything people ask before choosing their first Quatréa.

At 15% concentration, most people get six to nine hours on skin. Récolte lasts longest, Éveil the shortest but with a clean, close wear. Applying to moisturised skin and pulse points helps any perfume last longer.

Start with the moment you love most: open windows (Éveil), salt air (Zénith), woodsmoke (Récolte) or fresh snow (Givre). The collection page compares strength and character, or you can book a free consult and try all four.

Each perfume comes in a 10 ml discovery size (Rs 3,500), a 50 ml bottle (Rs 18,500) and a 100 ml bottle (Rs 27,500). All glass bottles are refillable, and refills are 15% less than a new bottle.

Yes. Visit the atelier for a free 20-minute consult, or order a discovery set of all four 10 ml bottles. If you buy a full-size bottle within 30 days, we credit the cost of the discovery set.

We deliver across Pakistan in 2 to 4 working days (same day inside Karachi on orders before noon). International shipping is offered on request. Tracking is sent by email and SMS.

Unopened bottles can be returned within 14 days for a full refund. Because perfume is personal, opened bottles can be exchanged once within 14 days if you are not happy with how it wears.

We do. Bespoke blends take four to eight weeks, and gift collections start from 20 boxes. See our case studies, then get in touch and we will reply within 24 hours.
