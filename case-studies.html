Scents with a story.

Three sample stories showing how we take a memory, a place or a moment and turn it into a scent. These are concept examples written for this site.

Bridal commission · Lahore

A garden for a wedding

  • 5 weeks
  • 12 trials
  • 60 favours
The challenge
A bride wanted her wedding to smell like her grandmother's garden after rain, but every floral she tried felt either too sweet or too formal.
Our approach
We spent five weeks building a one-off blend from jasmine, tuberose and a wet-stone accord, testing it on her skin, her mother's and her sister's. The final version borrows the green freshness of Éveil and adds a warmer floral heart.
The result
Sixty 10 ml favours, each with a hand-lettered label. Guests asked where to buy it, so the bride now has a private bottle on our shelf.

Hospitality · 38 rooms

A scent for a seaside hotel

  • 6 months
  • 38 rooms
  • 3 formats
The challenge
A boutique hotel wanted guests to feel the sea from the moment they stepped into the lobby, without the usual synthetic coconut.
Our approach
We stretched the citrus and salt of Zénith into a lighter room-diffuser formula, and built a linen spray and a take-home candle in the same family so the scent followed guests from lobby to pillow.
The result
A signature that staff describe as "the smell of arriving". The candle became the hotel's most-requested souvenir.

Corporate gifting · 120 boxes

Gifts for a design studio

  • 120 boxes
  • 3 cities
  • 4 weeks
The challenge
A design studio wanted a year-end gift that felt personal rather than corporate, and that would not end up forgotten in a drawer.
Our approach
Each box held a 10 ml Givre, a pressed-linen sachet and a handwritten card from the team member closest to that client. We packed everything in recycled board, printed with the studio's own pattern.
The result
Every box delivered on time across three cities, and the studio now re-orders each November.
