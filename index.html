A perfume for every kind of light.

Perfume behaves differently in January than in July. Heat lifts citrus and dulls spice; cold holds on to wood and amber. We composed each Quatréa for the air it will be worn in.

Éveil

Spring: Pear blossom, green tea, white musk

The first warm morning of the year, bottled. Éveil opens with pear blossom and bergamot, settles into peony and green tea, and dries down to clean white musk.

Zénith

Summer: Blood orange, sea salt, driftwood

Sun at its highest point. Zénith pairs blood orange and sea salt with creamy frangipani, then rests on warm driftwood and vanilla.

Autumn: Cardamom, fig leaf, amber

Harvest and woodsmoke. Récolte opens on cardamom and apple skin, deepens into cinnamon bark and rose, and settles on amber, patchouli and tobacco leaf.

Éveil

Spring. Cool light and open windows.

Éveil is named for awakening. We composed it over a single March, walking the same garden every dawn and writing down what changed: the green sharpness of new stems, the sweetness of the first blossom, the cool of wet stone.

  • Pear blossom
  • Bergamot
  • Peony
  • White musk
Discover Éveil

Zénith

Summer. Salt air and long afternoons.

Zénith is the noon scent: bright, salty and a little bit indulgent. We wanted citrus that stayed alive for hours instead of vanishing in ten minutes, so the orange is anchored to a mineral, sun-baked accord.

  • Blood orange
  • Yuzu
  • Neroli
  • Driftwood
Discover Zénith

Récolte

Autumn. Woodsmoke and slow evenings.

Récolte is the scent of the evening everyone goes indoors: warm spices, ripe fruit, a fire somewhere in the distance. It is the richest of our four, built around a deep amber that lasts well into the next morning.

  • Cardamom
  • Apple skin
  • Cinnamon bark
  • Amber
Discover Récolte

Givre

Winter. Clear air and fresh snow.

Givre means hoarfrost, the thin white crust that appears overnight on a window. We chased that feeling: crisp at first, then surprisingly soft, with a glow of incense underneath like a lamp left on in a quiet house.

  • Juniper
  • Cold pepper
  • Iris
  • White cedar
Discover Givre

From petal to bottle, slowly.

Gathered at first light

Petals, peels and resins reach the atelier within hours of picking, from growers we have worked with for years.

Steeped slowly

Every material macerates in organic alcohol for six weeks. No shortcuts and no rush.

Blended by hand

Our perfumer builds each season in layers, then tests it on skin in the weather it is named for.

Rested for sixty days

The blend sleeps in the dark so its notes settle into one calm, continuous scent.
